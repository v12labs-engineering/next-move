# India software job volume and feed options

Checked November 16, 2026. The target is distinct, open employer postings for experienced software roles anywhere in India, including remote roles that explicitly accept India candidates. The goal is new qualified jobs beyond our current catalog. Vendor totals and raw query results measure different things.

## Direct employer benchmark

The local database held 194 active India software jobs, with 52 posted in the past week. Thirty-three of 53 enabled feeds contributed India jobs. There were no SmartRecruiters feeds enabled. The case-insensitive employer-name check found no locally active India listings from the core benchmark employers.

**Amazon.** Four bounded reads of the India Software Development search returned 361 distinct requisition IDs. Each had the India country code and Software Development category. The role filter used the title and job description, excluding explicit intern, trainee, graduate and similar titles. Dates use the employer's date-only `posted_date` and calendar days. Of the 31 roles in the past week, 24 contained a numerical 2+ years signal in the basic software qualifications. The 30-day software roles included Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Gurugram, Chennai and Pune. **SmartRecruiters.** The documented `destination=PUBLIC`, `country=in` and `releasedAfter` filters yielded 560 India postings released in 30 days across Bosch, Eurofins, ServiceNow, Arista Networks, Western Digital, NielsenIQ, Experian, Version 1, Endava, Nagarro, NEC, H&M and QAD. Of those, 129 distinct posting IDs passed the clear-software-title filter after removing explicit entry-level titles. The rest include roles needing description-based classification. Thirteen selected recent detail records were active and public. Nine contained a numerical experience requirement of at least two years in their qualifications. Release dates can change when an employer republishes an ad. Retain source IDs and check repeat observations before treating the stream as newly opened requisitions. The recent feeds include Coimbatore, Pimpri-Chinchwad and India-remote options alongside the larger cities. ## Paid feed comparison

I found no feed around $10 per month that documents both production job-board use and direct employer application links. The lowest clear price for both was CleanJobData at $39 per month, with a 24-hour plan delay after indexing. Its public sample confirms relevant supply, and its overlap with India employer feeds remains to be measured. At the current budget, expand the verified direct employer feeds and measure the increase before paying for data.

## How to measure a pilot

1. Freeze a baseline of the existing India catalog. Count absolute additions and additions above baseline after merging canonical employer requisition IDs and URLs. One opening in several cities counts once.
2. Use the employer's original publication time for the seven-day count. Track ingestion time and checks separately. Hold out unknown original dates and do not treat reposts as new unless the employer issued a new requisition.
3. Verify India eligibility and classify software roles using the posting itself. Count jobs with explicit numerical 2+ requirements separately from obvious senior roles whose employer gives no number. Keep probable matches out of the verified count.
4. Check original application pages and collect removals. Record the percentage with usable application links and why individual links could not be verified. Repeat over a week to distinguish an initial backfill from ongoing fresh volume.

No provider account was created and no catalog rows were changed. No vendor-wide qualified yield can be inferred from the capped samples above.
