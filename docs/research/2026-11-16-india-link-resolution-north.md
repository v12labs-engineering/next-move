# STPI Noida employer link resolution

Checked 16 November 2026. The starting directory contained 519 registered-unit employer records in the Noida IT/ITeS and EHTP cohorts with neither a website nor a recruiting link. This pass records 118 website resolutions, including 99 recruiting links, against the original directory names. Within this starting cohort, 401 still need a verified website and 420 still need a recruiting link. The [resolution seed](../../deploy/local/india-employers/resolved-north.json) records each relationship with its own source URL. [STPI Noida IT/ITeS register](https://bhubaneswar.stpi.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/List%20of%20units%20registered%20with%20STPI%20Noida.pdf)

A bounded probe tested 87 selected company websites. It accepted 38 where the site displayed both the expected brand and India location evidence, including 26 where the same company page linked a careers destination. Other saved links came from direct checks of recruiting pages, company reports, government records and issuer announcements. Some destinations are company recruiting pages or India recruiting portals whose application links need a separate inventory check. The seed labels a parent or successor recruiting site where the legal employer differs from the brand operating the job board. For example, AMD publishes the Xilinx India corporate disclosures, LTM documents the merger of Mindtree, and NatWest Digital Services India records RBS Services India as its former name. Posting attribution should still use the employer named in each vacancy, and recruitment links shared by several legal entities refer to one board. GrapeCity India publishes its own careers site.
