# MyJobb competitor research

Checked 16 November 2026. This review covers public pages and maker statements. The onboarding form was inspected up to sign-in. Checkout, logged-in behavior, and applications remain untested.

## Positioning and audience

MyJobb leads with **“Be the first to every job.”** It sells an India-focused agent that finds a match, completes the application, and tracks the reply. The homepage illustrates ranked matches and a WhatsApp conversation before asking users to get early access. The sign-in page lists engineering, IT, data, and product roles. Its product facts describe an initial audience with 0–5 years of experience, while the public jobs directory and IIM Jobs announcement show broader coverage. The operator is Infyn Technologies, founded by Jhalak Gupta and Shreyansh Jain. [Homepage](https://myjobb.ai/) · [Sign-in](https://myjobb.ai/login-app) · [Product facts](https://myjobb.ai/ai-info) · [Changelog](https://myjobb.ai/changelog) · [About](https://myjobb.ai/about)

The localization shows up in the work the agent takes on. It handles Indian portals and fields such as current and expected compensation and notice period, shows prices in rupees, accepts UPI, and uses WhatsApp for application updates and questions. The product facts describe international coverage as a 2027 roadmap item. [Product facts](https://myjobb.ai/ai-info) · [Screening and application flow](https://myjobb.ai/features/auto-apply) · [Pricing](https://myjobb.ai/pricing)

## Product flow and trust

1. **Sign in and match.** The public CTA leads to Google sign-in. MyJobb says it scores jobs against the candidate’s resume and preferences, explains the score, and supports freshness filters and exclusions. [Sign-in](https://myjobb.ai/login-app) · [Matching](https://myjobb.ai/features/ai-job-matching)
2. **Connect and choose.** The public flow describes one-job and top-matches batch approval. The extension connects an existing job-board session. An October update describes phone or email code login for Naukri and phone codes for Hirist and IIM Jobs, with Instahyre still requiring the extension. [Auto-apply](https://myjobb.ai/features/auto-apply) · [Changelog](https://myjobb.ai/changelog)
3. **Apply and pause for missing information.** The vendor documents applications through five Indian portals (Naukri, Foundit, Hirist, IIM Jobs, Instahyre) and employer forms on Workday, Greenhouse, and Ashby. It describes Workday account creation, verification emails through a MyJobb inbox, and screening answers drawn from profile data, prior answers, or AI at a stated confidence threshold. A required unresolved answer pauses the run for email or WhatsApp input. [Auto-apply](https://myjobb.ai/features/auto-apply) · [Product facts](https://myjobb.ai/ai-info)
4. **Show the result.** The application log shows individual steps, the provenance of each answer, and a submitted status after the portal confirms it. Cases without confirmation go to review. The tracker associates employer replies with applications, and users can separately tailor resumes, find likely referrers, and draft or send outreach. [Auto-apply](https://myjobb.ai/features/auto-apply) · [Automation](https://myjobb.ai/features/automations) · [Features](https://myjobb.ai/features)

MyJobb says job-board sessions can be revoked and cookies are encrypted at rest. Its October changelog describes automatically checking a range of employer consents, including background checks and interview recording. Its terms disclaim guarantees of interviews, offers, or hires. These are disclosures about the vendor’s practices. [Privacy policy](https://myjobb.ai/privacy) · [Changelog](https://myjobb.ai/changelog) · [Terms, sections 5–6](https://myjobb.ai/terms-of-service)

- **Coverage.** The homepage advertises 15+ boards and applicant tracking systems. The product facts page enumerates 11 discovery sources with eight described submission integrations. Current detailed pages exclude LinkedIn from discovery and submission, while an older indexed auto-apply description included LinkedIn in the feed. The July changelog announced Lever discovery, and the current product facts exclude it. ## Acquisition and outcome evidence

MyJobb publishes searchable job pages by role and city, guides for questions such as how to automate applications in India, and a comparison hub for shoppers evaluating other tools. The site cross-links that content with feature pages and the free application offer. It also links to its public WhatsApp channel, and founder LinkedIn and Product Hunt posts show launch promotion. Those are observable acquisition channels. Their share of traffic or paid customers remains unknown. [Browse jobs](https://myjobb.ai/jobs/browse) · [Guides](https://myjobb.ai/guides) · [Comparisons](https://myjobb.ai/compare) · [WhatsApp channel](https://www.whatsapp.com/channel/0029Vb9glfZBlHpleUAQ0B3x) · [Founder launch post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jhalakkkk_were-live-on-product-hunt-this-is-probably-activity-7511055349764837376-DiAR) · [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/myjobb-ai)

The homepage reports 1M+ professionals and 400K+ applications, variously described as processed or sent. The founder says the earlier discovery product reached 600K+ users organically before it was rebuilt and that the replacement drew a 12K+ waitlist. These are self-reported counts, and the homepage does not define a period or denominator for application or hiring outcomes. Public sources reviewed did not establish active or paid users, trial conversion, application confirmation rate, interviews, hires, revenue, or cost per submission. ## What we should test for this product

MyJobb is a direct comparison for domestic engineering search in India and an application workflow benchmark more generally. Its own [coverage guide](https://myjobb.ai/ai-info) says international coverage is not live. Our baseline is a software engineering catalogue for domestic and international search, with a specific standard for [role-level sponsorship evidence](../../CONTEXT.md). That creates a positioning hypothesis, not proof of demand or of enough suitable supply.

| Decision | Evidence in this repo | Recommended next test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Positioning | The matching policy checks destination, work mode and sponsorship restrictions; it distinguishes employer sponsorship eligibility from evidence in the job itself. [Matching and tailoring](../../README.md#matching-and-tailoring) | Test a narrow promise: “Find software engineering roles abroad that fit your profile. See the sponsorship evidence, tailor your application, and track what was actually submitted.” Start with one role family and one destination where we can show enough fresh jobs. |
| First useful result | The root route redirects to Jobs. The application workflow already tailors for a selected role, uses grounded answers, and asks for missing required information. Final success requires an employer receipt. [Root route](../../app/page.tsx) · [Application contract](../application-pipeline.md#tailor--apply-interaction-contract) | Show a short demo: real job and match reasons → sponsorship evidence or unknown → tailored application → employer confirmation (or a clear unresolved status). Make the first 3–5 eligible submissions a no-card pilot; keep the candidate in control of which jobs are sent. |
| Messaging and follow-up | We have Telegram notices and employer code handling; the follow-up flow offers suggested contacts and drafts for the candidate to send. [Telegram](../telegram-notifications.md) · [Follow-up](../application-pipeline.md#application-follow-up) | Feature the existing status and blocker alerts in the demo. Measure how often people miss a required action before prioritizing another channel or automatic outreach. |
| Pricing | The current release plan still gates paid billing on checkout, usage records and entitlements. [Public launch gates](../production-release.md#public-launch-gates) | Test one prepaid pass with an allowance for **confirmed submissions**. Keep internal attempt caps to bound retry costs. Compare willingness to pay against the ₹999 benchmark, and set the allowance only after measuring total browser, model and support cost per confirmed submission, including unsuccessful attempts. |

For acquisition, test a few public pages for a specific destination and role only where the catalogue can show fresh openings and explain the sponsorship evidence. Connect each page to the first relevant shortlist. Track shortlist → chosen job → confirmed submission → recruiter reply or interview, with a defined denominator at each stage. Those results would be more useful for choosing price and volume than copying MyJobb's advertised quotas.
