# How job boards build their inventory

Checked 17 November 2026. Large boards get jobs from employers and recruiters who publish directly, from applicant tracking systems (ATSs) and distribution partners, and from public job pages they index. These channels can carry the same opening. Recruit Holdings documents all three and reports more than 20 million jobs searchable on Indeed, measured as a worldwide daily average in the 12 months through December 2025. [Recruit Holdings financial results, pages 9 and 11](https://recruit-holdings.com/en/ir/library/upload/recruit_202603Q4_earnings_en/)

## What a smaller product can observe

- **ATS partner feeds exist.** Ashby can provision a dedicated JSON or XML feed. After an employer opts into that partner's integration, Ashby sends its published jobs and updates the feed hourly. The number of India employers in any such feed depends on who opts in. [Ashby partner feeds](https://developers.ashbyhq.com/docs/dedicated-partner-job-feeds)
- **Job indexing starts with the posting URL.** Google documents structured `JobPosting` markup on individual job pages with fields for the employer, posting date, and expiry when applicable. Its Indexing API allows site owners to notify Google about additions, changes, and removals. Those fields let a collector validate a known job without first finding its employer's careers homepage. [JobPosting structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/job-posting) · [Indexing API](https://developers.google.com/search/apis/indexing-api/v3/quickstart)
- **MyJobb names a mix of sources.** The India-focused product lists eight job sites, including Naukri, Foundit and Indeed, and employer careers pages on Workday, Greenhouse and Ashby. Its public browse page displayed **68,320 live jobs** across sales, software, HR and other roles in the snapshot reviewed. That is MyJobb's displayed count. Its source mix by volume, collection method, deduplication and publisher agreements are undisclosed in the pages reviewed. [MyJobb source list](https://myjobb.ai/ai-info) · [Public jobs directory](https://myjobb.ai/jobs/browse)
- **India's National Career Service uses the same two intake routes.** Its employer FAQ describes direct posting, and its job seeker FAQ explains that partners supply additional listings that send applicants to the partner's site. The FAQ documents inbound partnership supply. [National Career Service FAQ](https://www.ncs.gov.in/job-seeker/_layouts/15/ncsp/faqs.aspx)

I tested the Apollo connector's free organization lookup (`apollo_organizations_lookup`) on six active registry names without a website or careers lead. The sample took two software or infotech names from each of Karnataka, Maharashtra and Telangana by stable hash of the registration ID. The full legal names returned no candidates. Shorter names returned two candidates without a domain or website URL. The Infosys control returned `infosys.com`. The result is **0 of 6 website leads** on this small sample. It supports using this lookup selectively and leaves national coverage undetermined. The six names were Elevyn Infotech, Athrill Software Solutions, Timbalore Software Technology, Tadnya Infotech, Unikdx Software Services and Plan-B Software Solutions. [Apollo connector documentation](https://docs.apollo.io/docs/apollo-mcp)

A search API can find the company and its ATS when the name is ambiguous or the employer appears in a fresh posting. Common Crawl's URL index can surface careers paths for an employer whose domain is already known. The previous Overture and Kerala tests show that public bulk and sector directories can contribute candidate domains with retained evidence. [Brave Search](https://brave.com/search/api/) · [Common Crawl URL index](https://commoncrawl.org/url-index) · [Local enrichment tests](2026-11-16-india-registry-enrichment-sources.md)

## First test for the catalog

1. **Compare JobsPikr and LinkUp on the same brief.** Request an active India software snapshot and seven days of additions and removals. The sample should have employer, title, location, original posting date when available, source URL, original apply URL and requisition ID when available. Check that each agreement covers the intended display and application workflow. Techmap is a useful comparison if the direct feeds leave material gaps. [JobsPikr feed fields](https://www.jobspikr.com/blog/connecting-jobspikr-to-job-boards-using-xml-feeds-to-automate-job-posting/) · [LinkUp trial](https://www.linkup.com/use-cases/linkup-job-listing-feeds)
2. **Measure usable additions against the current catalog.** Apply the same software and India rules. Deduplicate using the employer's requisition ID or canonical posting URL. Check that the job is still open and that the original employer application page works. Track a confirmed posting date separately from the provider's first-seen time. Compare vendors on unique usable jobs added, refresh and expiry behavior, and cost per usable addition. [Google job posting and expiry fields](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/job-posting) · [Catalog role filter](../../lib/server/catalog/feeds.ts)
3. **Use verified jobs to find more hiring employers.** Add employer ATS feeds with repeat supply to the existing source directory. Run a batch join with the PDL company file for the website gap and verify promising matches. Use targeted search for companies with hiring evidence when the bulk join fails. Each verified job can add a record to the catalog and point us to an employer worth monitoring.
