## What to measure in a pilot

Keep the employer sources as the record for live status and original publication dates. A permitted board feed can supplement them. Check unique employer postings after removing overlaps, roles with comparable location and experience evidence, and open original application links. The public feed samples above did not establish substantial additional fresh India software volume.

## Local pilot on November 16

- Shine’s robots rules allowed the checked search and detail paths. The first search page returned 20 listing links. Ten fetched detail pages had structured software postings, five of which Shine dated within seven days with unexpired listing dates. The five list experience minimums between four and 13 years. Titles, employers, locations, dates and source links are in the ignored local receipt under `.local-data/india-shine-pilot-2026-11-16T11-45-30-376Z.json`. The hourly catalog collects verified employer sources. Shine's terms distinguish automated access allowed by robots.txt from republication of its content. [Shine search](https://www.shine.com/job-search/fullstack-software-developer-jobs) · [Shine terms](https://www.shine.com/termsandconditions)
- Seven Remote Gig role pages yielded three supported employer feeds. HighLevel and JumpCloud had verified India software supply within 30 days. The existing India seed includes both, and local cron run `480f1784-157f-459a-8fdf-7023cd298fba` completed at 17:15 IST, publishing seven HighLevel and two JumpCloud posts in India. None had a publication date in the past seven days. The Zapier feed had zero India jobs under the collector’s location rules. [Remote Gig India category](https://remotegig.in/remote-jobs/open-to-india) · [HighLevel careers](https://jobs.lever.co/gohighlevel) · [JumpCloud careers](https://jobs.lever.co/jumpcloud)
