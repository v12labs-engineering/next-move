# India business registers for employer sourcing

Checked 16 November 2026. India publishes corporate, tax, MSME, and startup records through different systems. Ordinary partnership registration is administered through state-appointed Registrars of Firms. The Ministry of Statistics restricts its Statistical Business Register to authorized government agencies. Its public Sixth Economic Census directory covers a 2013–14 snapshot of establishments with at least ten workers and gives names, addresses and activity codes. We found no single public register that enumerates all businesses and their websites. [Indian Partnership Act, sections 56–59](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/9183/1/the_indian_partnership_act_1932.pdf) · [Ministry of Statistics access policy](https://mospi.gov.in/faq) · [Sixth Economic Census directory](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/6ec_dirEst/ec6_note_dir_esta.html)

The MCA preview returned one exact legal-name match each for Infosys Limited and Wipro Limited. Repeating the name filter returned only the last name. The UI schema exposed state, so a multi-name preview lookup is unverified. The NIC code was `NA` on both rows and the broad sectors differed from their familiar technology positioning. Industry alone is insufficient for deciding which companies might hire engineers. The open mirror below can be joined to our employer shortlist by defensive legal-name matching. Verified career URLs still come separately from employers. [RoC resource](https://www.data.gov.in/resource/registrars-companies-roc-wise-company-master-data)

The DPIIT historical PDF contained 114,961 consecutive rows and 114,960 distinct recognition IDs. Joining on normalized names produced 81 single-name matches against the saved India employer list. These are historical identity leads. Present recognition, hiring status, domains, and legal-name equivalence still require confirmation. Two requests through the Startup India directory's public search returned HTTP 503. [DPIIT roster](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/dam/invest-india/Templates/public/ListofStartups_03112023.pdf) · [Startup India search](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/search.html?roles=Startup&page=0)

We downloaded that official file and retained ten corporate fields for 7,353 distinct CINs. Its filename identifies March 2015, so it is a historical importer fixture. ## Public MCA snapshot

[companies.directory’s 18 October 2026 snapshot](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23485710) is a nationwide standardized mirror citing MCA RoC-wise Company Master Data via India’s Open Government Data portal. Its [public gzip download](https://zenodo.org/api/records/23485710/files/in-companies-official.csv.gz/content) requires no account or API key. The archive contains 12 columns with registry ID, normalized legal name, status, incorporation date and registered address components. It has no website, career URL or industry field. This is a separate snapshot from the current OGD row count. The separate MCA LLP and foreign-company registers are outside this file’s published scope.

The verified 291,430,363-byte archive is saved as `.local-data/india-business-registers/mca-companies-directory-2026-10-18.csv.gz`. It matches Zenodo’s MD5 `0e19631eb5fc017b409a0f591aec629d` and the publisher’s SHA-256 `173f23fddeea16a49c106f75a9606cafd240367af0ab7f34935876c41177a785`. A full CSV parse found 3,192,604 rows, all with source `ww-in`, country `IN`, a legal name and a registry ID. The registry IDs remain distinct after trimming and converting to uppercase. There are 2,154,810 records marked `Active` and 901,935 marked `Strike Off`. Fifty-five registry IDs fall outside the standard CIN pattern.
