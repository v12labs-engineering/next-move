# Apply access on 13 scraped India career sites

Checked live on 19 November 2026, one India posting per employer. **Five required sign-in or signup. Six offered a way to start as a guest. Two started with an email or candidate-profile check whose later screens remain unverified.** The site sample was chosen across recruiting platforms and is too small and selective to estimate the catalog-wide share.

A guest route reached a manual form before any login or signup, or explicitly said that no account was needed. Two guest forms showed a Submit control. Three had a Next control, and Nokia began with an email field. The inspection ended at the visible pre-submission screens. No candidate information was entered and no application was submitted.
