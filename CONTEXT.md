# Job search

The product serves domestic and international job seekers. This glossary
distinguishes destination choice from observed job supply and sponsorship evidence.

## Language

**Shared job catalogue**:
The common supply of sourced software engineering listings across destinations
and seniority levels, from CTO to junior developers and software interns.
Software leadership, architecture, DevOps/SRE, AI/data engineering and software
test automation are in scope. Non-software occupations are excluded independently
of the employer's industry. Ambiguous titles require software-work evidence.
Catalogue membership does not make a listing relevant to every candidate.

**Candidate recommendations**:
Jobs relevant to the logged-in candidate's profession, resume evidence,
experience and confirmed search preferences.
_Avoid_: Shared catalogue as a synonym for a candidate's recommendations.

**Selectable destination**:
A country or territory a candidate can choose for their search. Its availability
in the picker does not establish job coverage there.

**Domestic job search**:
A candidate's search for work within their current country.

**International job search**:
A candidate's search for work in another country. International location alone
does not establish sponsorship or eligibility to work there.

**Confirmed role-level sponsorship**:
Explicit evidence in a job posting that sponsorship is offered for that role and
applicable destination. An employer's sponsor-register entry alone is insufficient.
_Avoid_: Sponsor employer as a substitute for a sponsorship-confirmed vacancy.

**Unknown sponsorship**:
A posting whose available evidence does not establish whether the role offers
sponsorship. It is shown with a caveat and counted separately from confirmed
role-level sponsorship.

## Job supply

**Source candidate**:
An observed career-page or feed identity awaiting validation. Its presence does
not establish a working source or any available jobs.

**Collection source**:
A validated employer board, career site or feed with a defined inventory scope.
One employer can have several collection sources across platforms or regions.
_Avoid_: Employer or individual job as a synonym for source.

**Complete source snapshot**:
An observation of the entire inventory exposed within a collection source's
declared scope. A search window, incomplete page sequence or truncated result
does not establish the whole employer inventory.

**Source observation**:
Evidence that a source or listing was checked at a recorded time. A new
observation does not necessarily mean a new or changed job.

**Catalogue change**:
A sourced addition, material update, reopening or confirmed closure of a listing.
A repeated observation of unchanged content is not a catalogue change.

**Source freshness**:
The age of the last successful complete observation within the source's declared
scope. Starting a check does not make the source fresh.
