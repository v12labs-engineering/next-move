# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Primarily skilled professionals (mid/senior engineers and specialists) hunting visa-sponsored roles abroad. They check in daily, work through a queue of matches, and carry real timeline pressure (visa windows, notice periods, savings runway). Secondary: early-career movers still deciding which countries are realistic; they need the same tool to stay approachable, not a separate mode.

## Product Purpose
Turn an anxious, scattered relocation search into a fast daily loop: see new matches ranked against your experience, judge visa likelihood at a glance, act (apply, tailor resume, dismiss), and track outcomes. Success is a user who trusts the ranking and visa signal enough to act in seconds, and who feels momentum toward a move.

## Brand Personality
Ambitious, energetic, precise. The product feels like forward motion: crisp, quick, a little playful in its polish, never noisy. Voice is direct and encouraging without hype; it states uncertainty plainly (visa signals, stale sources) instead of hiding it.

## Anti-references
- LinkedIn / Indeed: cluttered job-board feeds, ads, noise, endless identical cards.
- Generic AI SaaS: purple gradients, glass cards, gradient text, hero-metric tiles.
- Government and visa portals: bureaucratic, form-heavy, intimidating.
- Gamified or childish: streaks, confetti, mascots.

## Design Principles
1. **Momentum over browsing.** Every screen should push toward the next action (apply, tailor, dismiss), not toward scrolling.
2. **Signal at a glance.** Fit and visa confidence are the two decisions; make them readable before the title is.
3. **Honest uncertainty.** Show what is unknown or stale clearly and calmly; trust is the product.
4. **Keyboard-fast, mouse-friendly.** Power users should fly; newcomers should never feel lost.
5. **Polish is energy.** Delight comes from precise motion and tactile feedback, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Visa/fit status never conveyed by color alone (always label + icon). Full keyboard navigation with visible focus. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
